QuickDL Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

What QuickDL stores

QuickDL creates an opaque browser identifier so your credit balance, download history and User ID can remain available when you return. If you connect Google, QuickDL stores the Google account identifier, verified email, display name and profile image needed to maintain that connection.

Downloads

QuickDL is designed for publicly accessible media. Download jobs, status and basic job metadata may be stored for a limited period so the service can complete the download and show your history.

Payments

PayPal handles payment details. QuickDL stores payment/order references and the credits granted after a completed payment; it does not store your PayPal password or card details.

Cookies

QuickDL uses a first-party cookie named vexdou_visitor to keep your account stable. The cookie is not a tracking advertisement cookie.

Contact

Use the Contact & support page to report problems or send feedback.

Public media limitation

QuickDL does not provide a method for bypassing private accounts, DRM, CAPTCHA or login/access controls.
